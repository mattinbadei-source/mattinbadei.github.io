The restriction on wearing hijabs in school and what has resulted

1.Why did they do it?

The Azerbaijani government implemented the school hijab restriction to enforce and protect the country’s constitutional principle of secular education.

Through a Cabinet of Ministers amendment passed on September 21, 2026 , Azerbaijan updated its state school uniform guidelines to a strictly unified dress code. Authorities argue that enforcing a neutral, unified appearance ensures that state general education institutions maintain a classroom environment free from religion.

2. Indirect Enforcement

The updated regulations do not explicitly mention the word hijab . But they prohibit any additional clothing that isn’t from the official uniform or "violates the principle of secularism".

3.Secular State Identity

Despite having a population that is roughly 96% Muslim , the Azerbaijani constitution separates religion from the state. The ruling party, the Yeni Azerbaijan Party, defends these measures as necessary steps to uphold the republic's secularism.

4. Resistance and Backlash

The enforcement has sparked national debate and led to the largest public demonstrations the country has seen in years

a. Class Boycotts:

Students and parents have staged protests outside school gates, and some pupils have boycotted lessons in solidarity with peers who were refused entry.

The issue concerning female students needs to be discussed. Public schools should establish certain compromise arrangements for primary school pupils... Teachers are adults, and imposing restrictions on how they dress is the wrong approach. says Natiq Jafarli (Head of the Opposition REAL Party)

b. Arrests

The government reported that at least 37 people have been detained for public order violations, following clashes and attempts to damage educational facilities

c. Geopolitical Friction

The secular policy has also caused friction with its close ally, Turkey.
